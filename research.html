Research

PhD student researcher in the Computational Imaging and Robotic Perception (CIRP) Lab.

Current Role

I am a PhD student in the Computational Imaging and Robotic Perception (CIRP) Lab, where my work explores perception-driven intelligence for robotics and embodied systems.

My research includes Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models for connecting visual understanding, language reasoning, and action planning in real-world environments.

Design and Fabrication

Alongside model research, I fabricate and prototype system components by creating designs in CAD software and producing parts with 3D printing workflows.

This allows fast iteration between software experiments and physical system development.

Academic Progress

I am currently pursuing my Masters degree, and it is expected that I will attain this degree in Fall 2026.
